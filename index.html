OUR MOTIVE & PHILOSOPHY

Bridging Theory & Industry Reality

InternWell, the upskilling forum, operates with a primary mission to equip students with valuable industrial experience by offering them the chance to engage in live freelancing projects.

The forum recognizes the significance of bridging the gap between theoretical learning and practical application, ensuring that students can effectively adapt to the evolving demands of the industry. Through hands-on involvement in real-world projects, students gain a deeper understanding of industry practices, enhance their problem-solving skills, and foster creativity. This exposure to professional environments nurtures their talents, helping them emerge as well-rounded individuals with a comprehensive skill set.

FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Answers & Clarifications

What is InternWell SLIET and who can join?
InternWell is a Socio-Startup and upskilling technical forum at Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET). Any student across any department (CSE, ECE, EIE, MECH, CIVIL, FOOD TECH, CHE) passionate about coding, design, freelancing projects, or management is welcome to join!
What kind of live projects do interns work on?
Interns work on tailored websites, web applications, and digital solutions for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) as well as campus utilities, gaining authentic freelancing and industrial development experience.
What is Coding Spardha?
Coding Spardha is our annual flagship coding hackathon conducted in collaboration with the SLIET Software Development Club (SSDC), featuring events like Web-O-Design, Code Manthan, and the famous Dark Code challenge.
What is the I-MAT exam?
I-MAT (InternWell Mental Ability Test) is an objective-type competitive exam featuring 100 aptitude questions sourced from challenging exams to prepare students for campus placements and technical job assessments.
How can I contact the club leadership?
You can email us at internwellclub@gmail.com, follow us on Instagram @internwell.sliet, or meet our coordinators on the SLIET Longowal campus!
